sue	SUE	v.t.	To seek justice or right from one by legal process; to institute process in law against one; to prosecute in a civil action for the recovery of a real or supposed right; as, to sue one for debt; to sue one for damages in trespass. Matthew 5:40.	To gain by legal process.	To clean the beak, as a hawk; a term of falconry. To sue out, to petition for and take out; or to apply for and obtain; as, to sue out a writ in chancery; to sue out a pardon for a criminal.
sue	SUE	v.i.	To prosecute; to make legal claim; to seek for in law; as, to sue for damages.	To seek by request; to apply for; to petition; to entreat. By adverse destiny constrain'd to sue For counsel and redress, he sues to you.	To make interest for; to demand. Caesar come to Rome to sue for the double honor of a triumph and the consulship.
sued	SUED	pp.	Prosecuted; sought in law.
suet	SUET	n.	The fat of an animal, particularly that about the kidneys; lard.
suety	SUETY	a.	Consisting of suet, or resembling it; as a suety substance.

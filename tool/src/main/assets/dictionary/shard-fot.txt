fother	FOTHER	n.	[See Food.] A weight of lead containing eight pigs, and every pig twenty one stone and a half. But the fother is of different weights. With the plumbers in London it is nineteen hundred and a half, and at the mines, it is twenty two hundred and a half.
fother	FOTHER	v.t.	To endeavor to stop a leak in the bottom of a ship, while afloat, by letting down a sail by the corners, and putting chopped yarn, oakum, wool, cotton, etc. Between it and the ship's sides. These substances are sometimes sucked into the cracks and the leak stopped
fothering	FOTHERING	ppr.	Stopping leaks, as above.
fothering	FOTHERING	n.	The operation of stopping leaks in a ship, as above.

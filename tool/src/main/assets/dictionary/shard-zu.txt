zuffolo	ZUFFOLO	n.	A little flute or flageolet, especially that which is used to teach birds
zumate	ZUMATE	n.	[See Zumic.] A combination of the zumic acid and a salifiable base.
zumic	ZUMIC	a.	The zumic acid is procured from many accescent vegetable substances
zumological	ZUMOLOGICAL	a.	[See Zumology.] Pertaining to zumology.
zumologist	ZUMOLOGIST	n.	One who is skilled in the fermentation of liquors.
zumology	ZUMOLOGY	n.	A treatise on the fermentation of liquors, or the doctrine of fermentation
zumosimeter	ZUMOSIMETER	n.	An instrument proposed by Swammerdam for ascertaining the degree of fermentation occasioned by the mixture of different liquids, and the degree of heat which they acquire in fermentation
zurlite	ZURLITE	n.	A newly discovered Vesuvian mineral, whose primitive form is a cube, or according to some authors, a rectangular prism.

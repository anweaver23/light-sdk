ghost	GHOST	n.	Spirit; the soul of man. In this sense seldom used. But hence,	The soul of a deceased person; the soul or spirit separate from the body; an apparition. The mighty ghosts of our great Harrys rose. To give up the ghost is to die; to yield up the breath or spirit; to expire. The Holy ghost is the third person in the adorable Trinity.
ghost	GHOST	v.i.	To die; to expire.
ghost	GHOST	v.t.	To haunt with an apparition.

ghostliness	GHOSTLINESS	n.	Spiritual tendency. [Little used.]
ghostly	GHOSTLY	a.	Spiritual; relating to the soul; not carnal or secular. Save and defend us from our ghostly enemies.	Spiritual; having a character from religion; as a ghostly father.	Pertaining to apparitions.

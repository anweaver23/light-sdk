yttria	YTTRIA	n.	One of the earths. It has the appearance of a fine white powder, without taste or smell. It is insoluble in water, and does not affect vegetable blues. It combines with acids and forms salts. Its base is yttrium
yttrious	YTTRIOUS	a.	Pertaining to yttria; containing yttria; as the yttrious oxyd of columbium.
yttrium	YTTRIUM	n.	The base of yttria.
yttro-cerite	YTTRO-CERITE	n.	A mineral consisting of the oxyd of cerium, yttria, lime and fluoric acid.
yttro-columbite	YTTRO-COLUMBITE	n.	A mineral containing yttria.
yttro-tantalite	YTTRO-TANTALITE	n.	A mineral found in kidney-form masses; an ore of tantalum.

exfoliate	EXFOLIATE	v.i.	In surgery and mineralogy, to separate and come off in scales, as pieces of carious bone; to scale off, as the lamins of a mineral
exfoliated	EXFOLIATED	pp.	Separated in thin scales, as a carious bone.
exfoliating	EXFOLIATING	ppr.	Separating and coming off in scales.
exfoliation	EXFOLIATION	n.	The scaling of a bone; the process of separating, as pieces of unsound bone from the sound part; desquamation.
exfoliative	EXFOLIATIVE	a.	That has the power of causing exfoliation or the desquamation of a bone.
exfoliative	EXFOLIATIVE	n.	That which has the power or quality of procuring exfoliation.

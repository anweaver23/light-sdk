bbarbacan	BBARBACAN	n.	A fortification or outer defense to a city or castle, consisting of an elevation of earth about three feet high, along the foot of the rampart.	A fortress at the end of a bridge, or at the outlet of a city, having a double wall with towers.	An opening in the wall of a fortress through which guns are leveled and fired upon an enemy. The French use the word also for an aperture in a wall to let in or drain off water; and the Spaniards, for a low wall round a church yard.
bbarbarism	BBARBARISM	n.	An offense against purity of style or language; any form of speech contrary to the pure idioms of a particular language.	Ignorance of arts; want of learning.	Rudeness of manners; savagism; incivility; ferociousness; a savage state of society.	Brutality; cruelty; barbarity. [In this sense little used, being superseded by barbarity.]

poem	POEM	n.	A metrical composition; a composition in which the verses consist of certain measures, whether in blank verse or in rhyme; as the poems of Homer or of Milton; opposed to prose.	This term is also applied to some compositions in which the language is that of excited imagination; as the poems of Ossian.
poesy	POESY	n.	The art or skill of composing poems; as, the heavenly gift of poesy	Poetry; metrical composition. Music and poesy used to quicken you.	A short conceit engraved on a ring or other thing.
poet	POET	n.	The author of a poem; the inventor or maker of a metrical composition. A poet is a maker, as the word signifies; and he who cannot make, that is, invent, hath his name for nothing.	One skilled in making poetry, or who has a particular genius for metrical composition; one distinguished for poetic talents. Many write verses who cannot be called poets.
poet-laureat	POET-LAUREAT	n.	A poet employed to compose poems for the birth days of a prince or other special occasion.
poet-musician	POET-MUSICIAN	n.	An appellation given to the bard and lyrist of former ages, as uniting the professions of poetry and music.
poetaster	POETASTER	n.	A petty poet; a pitiful or rhymer or writer of verses.
poetess	POETESS	n.	A female poet.
poetic	POETIC		POET'ICAL, adjective [Latin poeticus.]	Pertaining to poetry; suitable to poetry; as a poetical genius; poetic turn or talent; poetic license.	Expressed in poetry or measure; as a poetical composition.	Possessing the peculiar beauties of poetry; sublime; as a composition or passage highly poetical.
poetical	POETICAL	a.	Pertaining to poetry; suitable to poetry; as a poetical genius; poetic turn or talent; poetic license.	Expressed in poetry or measure; as a poetical composition.	Possessing the peculiar beauties of poetry; sublime; as a composition or passage highly poetical.
poetically	POETICALLY	adv.	With the qualities of poetry; by the art of poetry; by fiction.
poetics	POETICS	n.	The doctrine of poetry.

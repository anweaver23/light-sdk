uglily	UGLILY	adv.	In an ugly manner; with deformity.
ugliness	UGLINESS	n.	Total want of beauty; deformity of person; as old age and ugliness	Turpitude of mind; moral depravity; lothesomeness. Their dull ribaldry must be offensive to any one who does not, for the sake of the sin, pardon the ugliness of its circumstances.
ugly	UGLY	a.	[See Hack.[ Deformed; offensive to the sight; contrary to beauty; hateful; as an ugly person; an ugly face. O I have pass'd a miserable night, so full of ugly sights, of ghastly dreams. Fellow, begone; I cannot bear thy sight; this news hath made thee a most ugly man.

kumiss	KUMISS	n.	A liquor or drink made from mare's milk fermented and distilled; milkspirit, used by the Tartars.
kuril	KURIL	n.	A bird, the black petrel.
kurilian	KURILIAN	a.	The kurilian isles are a chain in the Pacific, extending from the southern extremity of Kamschatka to Jesso.

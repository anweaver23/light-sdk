suability	SUABILITY	n.	Liability to be sued; the state of being subject by law to civil process. [Not much used.]
suable	SUABLE	a.	That may be sued; subject by law to be called to answer in court
suade	SUADE		for persuade, is not in use.
suage	SUAGE		for assuage, is not in use.
suant	SUANT	a.	Even; uniform; spread equally over the surface. [New England, but local.]
suasible	SUASIBLE	a.	That may be persuaded or easily persuaded
suasion	SUASION	n.	The act of persuading. [See Persuade.]
suasive	SUASIVE	a.	Having power to persuade
suasory	SUASORY	a.	Tending to persuade; having the quality of convincing and drawing by argument or reason
suavity	SUAVITY	n.	Sweetness, in a literal sense. [Not in use.]	Sweetness, in a figurative sense; that which is to the mind what sweetness is to the tongue; agreeableness; softness; pleasantness; as suavity of manners; suavity of language, conversation or address.

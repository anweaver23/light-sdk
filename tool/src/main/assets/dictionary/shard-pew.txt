pewter	PEWTER	n.	A composition or factitious metal, consisting of tin and lead, or tin, lead and brass, in the proportions of a hundred pounds of tin to fifteen of lead, and six of brass. This was formerly in extensive use in domestic utensils or vessels; but being a soft composition and easily melted, is now less used.	Vessels or utensils made of pewter; as plates, dishes, porringers and the like.
pewterer	PEWTERER	n.	One whose occupation is to make vessels and utensils of pewter.

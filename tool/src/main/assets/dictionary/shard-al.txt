The distinction in scripture between a parable and an allegory is said to be that a parable is a supposed history, and an allegory a figurative description of real facts. An allegory is called a continued metaphor. The following line in Virgil is an example of an allegory Claudite jam rivos, pueri, sat prata biberunt. Stop the currents, young men, the meadows have drank sufficiently; that is let your music cease, our ears have been sufficiently delighted

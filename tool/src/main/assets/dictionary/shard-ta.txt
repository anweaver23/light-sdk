To load with a burden or burdens. The narrator--never taxes our faith beyond the obvious bounds of probability.	To assess, fix or determine judicially, as the amount of cost on actions in court; as, the court taxes bills of cost.	To charge; to censure; to accuse; usually followed by with; as, to tax a man with pride. He was taxed with presumption. Men's virtues I have commended as freely as I have taxed their crimes. [To tax of a crime, is not in use, nor to tax for. Both are now improper.]
taxable	TAXABLE	a.	That may be taxed; liable by law to the assessment of taxes; as taxable estate. By the laws of some states, polls are taxable after the age of seventy.	That may be legally charged by a court against the plaintiff or defendant in a suit; as taxable costs.

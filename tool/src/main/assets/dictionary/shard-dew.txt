dew	DEW	n.	The water or moisture collected or deposited on or near the surface of the earth, during the night, by the escape of the heat which held the water in solution

dew-lap	DEW-LAP	n.	The flesh that hangs from the throat of oxen, which laps or licks the dew in grazing.	In Shakespeare, a lip flaccid with age.
dew-lapt	DEW-LAPT	a.	Furnished with a dew-lap.
dew-worm	DEW-WORM	n.	A worm, called otherwise earth-worm, a species of Lumbricus, which lives just under the surface of the ground. It is of a pale red color, and does no injury to plants.

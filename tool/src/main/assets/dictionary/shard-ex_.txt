ex	EX		A Latin preposition or prefix, Gr. signifying out of, out, proceeding from. Hence in composition, it signifies sometimes out of, as in exhale, exclude; sometimes off, from or out, as in Latin excindo, to cut off or out; sometimes beyond, as in excess, exceed, excel. In some words it is merely emphatical; in others it has little effect on the signification.
ex-commissary	EX-COMMISSARY	n.	A commissary dismissed from office; one formerly a commissary
ex-director	EX-DIRECTOR	n.	One who has been a director, but is displaced.
ex-legislator	EX-LEGISLATOR	n.	One who has been a legislator, but is not at present.
ex-minister	EX-MINISTER	n.	One who has been minister, but is not in office.
ex-prefect	EX-PREFECT	n.	A prefect out of office; one who has been a prefect and is displaced.
ex-president	EX-PRESIDENT	n.	One who has been president, but is no longer in the office.
ex-representative	EX-REPRESENTATIVE	n.	One who has been formerly a representative, but is no longer one.
ex-secretary	EX-SECRETARY	n.	One who has been secretary, but is no longer in office.
ex-senator	EX-SENATOR	n.	One who has been a senator, but is no longer one.

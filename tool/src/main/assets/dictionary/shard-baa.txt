baa	BAA	n.	The cry or appropriate bleating of sheep.
baa	BAA	v.i.	To cry or bleat as sheep.
baal	BAAL	n.	An idol among the ancient Chaldeans and Syrians, representing the sun. The word signifies also lord, or commander; and the character of the idol was varied by different nations, at different times. Thus baal Berith is supposed to signify the Lord of the Covenant; baal Peor, or rather baal Phegor, the Lord of the dead. Ps. cvi, baal Zebub, the god of flies, etc.

ununiform	UNUNIFORM	a.	Not uniform; wanting uniformity. [Little used.]
unupheld	UNUPHELD	a.	Not upheld; not sustained.
unurged	UNURGED	a.	Not urged; not pressed with solicitation.
unused	UNUSED	a.	Not put to use; not employed.	That has never been used.	Not accustomed; as hands unused to labor; hearts unused to deceit.
unuseful	UNUSEFUL	a.	Useless; serving no good purpose.
unusual	UNUSUAL	a.	s as z. Not usual; not common; rare; as an unusual season; a person of unusual graces or erudition.
unusually	UNUSUALLY	adv.	s as z. Not commonly; not frequently; rarely. This summer, 1828 has been unusually rainy.
unusualness	UNUSUALNESS	n.	s as z. Uncommonness; infrequency; rareness of occurrence.
unutterable	UNUTTERABLE	a.	That cannot be uttered or expressed; ineffable; inexpressible; as unutterable anguish; unutterable joy.

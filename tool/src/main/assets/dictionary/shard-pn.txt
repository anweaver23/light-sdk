pneumatic	PNEUMATIC		PNEUMATICAL, adjective numat'ic. [Gr. breath, spirit; to breathe or blow.]	Consisting of air, as a thin compressible substance; opposed to dense or solid substances. The pneumatic substance being, in some bodies, the native spirit of the body.	Pertaining to air, or to the philosophy of its properties; as pneumatic experiments; a pneumatic engine.	Moved or played by means of air; as a pneumatic instrument of music.
pneumatical	PNEUMATICAL	a.	Consisting of air, as a thin compressible substance; opposed to dense or solid substances. The pneumatic substance being, in some bodies, the native spirit of the body.	Pertaining to air, or to the philosophy of its properties; as pneumatic experiments; a pneumatic engine.	Moved or played by means of air; as a pneumatic instrument of music.
pneumatics	PNEUMATICS	n.	In natural philosophy, that branch which treats of air. In chimistry, that branch which treats of the gases.	In the schools, the doctrine of spiritual substances, as God, angels, and the souls of men.
pneumatocele	PNEUMATOCELE	n.	In surgery, a distension of the scrotum by air
pneumatological	PNEUMATOLOGICAL	a.	Pertaining to pneumatology.
pneumatologist	PNEUMATOLOGIST	n.	One versed in pneumatology.
pneumatology	PNEUMATOLOGY	n.	The doctrine of the properties of elastic fluids, or of spiritual substances.	A treatise on elastic fluids, or on spiritual substances.
pneumonia	PNEUMONIA		PNEUMON'IC, adjective Pertaining to the lungs; pulmonic.
pneumonic	PNEUMONIC	a.	Pertaining to the lungs; pulmonic.
pneumonic	PNEUMONIC	n.	A medicine for affections of the lungs.
pneumony	PNEUMONY	n.	In medicine, an inflammation of the lungs

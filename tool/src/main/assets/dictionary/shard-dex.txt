dexter	DEXTER	a.	Right, as opposed to left; a term used in heraldry, to denote the right side of a shield or coat of arms; as, bend-dexter; dexter-point
dexterity	DEXTERITY	n.	Readiness of limbs; adroitness; activity; expertness; skill; that readiness in performing an action, which proceeds from experience or practice, united with activity or quick motion. We say, a man handles an instrument, or eludes a thrust, with dexterity	Readiness of mind or mental faculties, as in contrivance, or inventing means to accomplish a purpose; promptness in devising expedients; quickness and skill in managing or conducting a scheme of operations. We say, a negotiation is conducted with dexterity
dextral	DEXTRAL	a.	Right, as opposed to left.
dextrality	DEXTRALITY	n.	The state of being on the right side.
dextrorsal	DEXTRORSAL	a.	Rising from right to left, as a spiral line or helix.
dextrous	DEXTROUS	a.	Ready and expert in the use of the body and limbs; skillful and active in manual employment; adroit; as a dextrous hand; a dextrous workman.	Ready in the use of the mental faculties; prompt in contrivance and management; expert; quick at inventing expedients; as a dextrous manager.	Skillful; artful; done with dexterity; as dextrous management.
dextrously	DEXTROUSLY	adv.	With dexterity; expertly; skillfully; artfully; adroitly; promptly.
dextrousness	DEXTROUSNESS	n.	Dexterity; adroitness.

fomahant	FOMAHANT	n.	A star of the first magnitude, in the constellation Aquarius.
foment	FOMENT	v.t.	To apply warm lotions to; to bathe with warm medicated liquors, or with flannel dipped in warm water.	To cherish with heat; to encourage growth. [Not usual.]	To encourage; to abet; to cherish and promote by excitements; in a bad sense; as, to foment ill humors. So we say, to foment troubles or disturbances; to foment intestine broils.
fomentation	FOMENTATION	n.	The act of applying warm liquors to a part of the body, by means of flannels dipped in hot water or medicated decoctions, for the purpose of easing pain, by relaxing the skin, or of discussing tumors.	The lotion applied, or to be applied to a diseased part.	Excitation; instigation, encouragement.
fomented	FOMENTED	pp.	Bathed with warm lotions; encouraged.
fomenter	FOMENTER	n.	One who foments; one who encourages or instigates; as a fomenter of sedition.
fomenting	FOMENTING	ppr.	Applying warm lotions.	Encouraging; abetting; promoting.

fop	FOP	n.	A vain man of weak understanding and much ostentation; one whose ambition is to gain admiration by showy dress and pertness; a gay trifling man; a coxcomb
fopdoodle	FOPDOODLE	n.	An insignificant fellow. [Vulgar and not used.]
fopling	FOPLING		A petty fop.
foppery	FOPPERY	n.	Affectation of show or importance; showy folly; as the foppery of dress or of manners.	Folly; impertinence. Let not the sound of shallow foppery enter my sober house.	Foolery; vain or idle practice; idle affectation.
foppish	FOPPISH	a.	Vain of dress; making an ostentatious display of gay clothing; dressing in the extreme of fashion.	Vain; trifling; affected in manners.
foppishly	FOPPISHLY	adv.	With vain ostentation of dress; in a trifling or affected manner.
foppishness	FOPPISHNESS	n.	Vanity and extravagance in dress; showy vanity.
